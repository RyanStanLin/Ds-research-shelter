# Factor feasibility status before full text

Final selected factors: **not determined**. The current directory links 38 reports to eleven candidate factors; two population-ineligible reports are kept in the screening ledger and excluded from the distribution counts. The same report can link to several factors, so factor totals overlap. Reviews and qualitative reports are context, not effect-size inputs. All confirmed independent-study and extractable-comparable-study counts remain NA.

| Candidate factor | Current evidence leads | Main obstacle to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Captioning | Presence/absence, combined signing, expanded/standard captions, edited/near-verbatim captions | These are different contrasts. Student status and educational content vary; many designs are within-person. |
| Sign language support | ASL versus SimCom, direct instruction versus mediated instruction, captions/signing combinations | Distinct sign languages, proficiency, and CI subgroups; repeated measures and overlapping institutional series. |
| Real-time transcription | C-Print/interpreting and lecture retention; RTC working-memory proxy | Few direct classroom publications; several experiments per report; clinical/working-memory proxies and qualitative accounts. |
| Assistive listening devices | Dynamic-classroom RM/CROS comparison; recognition studies and amplification review | Much evidence is word/sentence recognition, not content comprehension; distinguish technologies and true DHH cohorts. |
| Visual aids | Expanded captions with illustrations/maps; visual materials and interpreted teaching | Intervention bundles; vocabulary scores and visual-access descriptions cannot be pooled with lecture comprehension. |
| Teaching speed | Caption rate and reduction experiments | Retrieved rate studies primarily manipulate caption presentation, not teacher delivery speed; do not equate these. |
| Written materials | Lecture notes, printed text, expanded definitions | Notes as supplementary material differ from print as the teaching modality; distinguish delayed retention from immediate learning. |
| Teaching modality | Live/video, direct/mediated, print/ASL, video delivery methods | Requires specific common contrasts; not a single homogeneous factor label. |
| Background noise | CI students' content tests, UHL narrative comprehension, simulated classroom comprehension | Some studies compare hearing groups rather than noise conditions; recognition-only studies are near-scope. |
| Seating position | Listening distance/spatial configuration, dynamic talkers, review/context leads | No verified controlled classroom seat intervention yet; physical distance is an indirect lead and task layout is not exposure manipulation. |
| Visual accessibility | Competing visual sources, caption placement/color, visual orientation | Most context reports do not estimate comprehension effects of changing spatial access; gaze measures are not comprehension. |

After PDFs arrive, rank feasible **contrast families** using independently verified samples, complete effect statistics, similar outcome/timepoint constructs, defensible design handling and risk of bias. Do not choose a factor simply because its preliminary report-link count is highest. Additional focused searches and citation chasing may be needed after the initial full-text batch; they should be consolidated into as few further acquisition batches as possible.
