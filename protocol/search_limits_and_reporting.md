# Search record and reporting boundaries

Date: 14 October 2026, Asia/Taipei. Population, access, educational-content and environmental queries are archived in literature/searches. General academic discovery, site-targeted PubMed/ERIC queries, OpenAlex metadata and citation leads were used. These interfaces are not claimed to be native exhaustive exports.

The final curated directory has 43 publication records, including excluded and contextual records. The retrieval ledger has 389 result appearances, including repeated URLs, background pages, methods sources and nonacademic hits. An initial six queries were reconstructed from tool-call records but their returned lists were not captured. Therefore **389 is not a PRISMA records-identified denominator**, and the directory size is not the total number of papers found by a reproducible native search.

Exactly 19 original PDFs supplied by the user were assessed and hashed. The first batch requested 24 originals; five were not supplied. Other metadata-only publications were screened at abstract/index level and contribute no effect data. The workflow did not access unsupplied original PDFs. Original statistics were not filled from abstracts, reviews or secondhand citations.

Search novelty, purchase availability and a single AI-assisted screening/extraction workflow are limitations. The final manuscript transparently calls itself an evidence review and exploratory synthesis. It does not claim exhaustive searches, independent duplicate review, prospectively registered factor selection, or a completed PRISMA 2020 systematic review. PRISMA is used as reporting guidance, not a certification.
