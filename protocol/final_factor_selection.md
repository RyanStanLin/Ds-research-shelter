# Final factor selection

Assessment date: 2026-10-14. Decisions follow the supplied originals, not the preliminary tag counts.

The final core factors are **reading proficiency, captioning, and sign-language support**. Reading was added after source feasibility assessment and is observational. Caption and language-support comparisons remain separate. No omnibus “accessibility effect” is estimated.

| Factor | Evidence available for quantitative work | Comparability and quality | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading proficiency | P05 school and college r=.51/.63, n=48 each; P08 r=.75 at 120 wpm, n=20 | Three different cohorts, two reports. All measure reading-related ability and content comprehension; tests, support displays and review timing differ. P05 pairwise n not printed separately, derived from complete measurement; college composite includes four tests and four SAT verbal scores. | Primary **exploratory association** synthesis, DL Fisher z; conventional and modified KH intervals; rate/support/leave-one-out sensitivities. |

P13 first-period n25/26 contrast available as nonstudent context. | Addition, rate, editing and expansion are different interventions. P06 counts conflict; P07 paired posttest covariance absent; P10 cell n absent; P11 analyzed n/SD unclear. | Selected for standalone effect sizes, **no common pooled effect**. |
| Sign-language support | P15 overall paired t, n40; P16 mean/SD/r, n48 | SimCom is different from natural sign-language interpreting. ## Independence

Thirty reported DHH study units are registered, but not thirty globally verified independent samples. RIT report recruitment dates are mostly unavailable. The primary synthesis uses only two explicitly separate P05 cohorts plus the different Sydney P08 cohort. Within-cohort caption rates and outcome types never count as new studies. Sensitivities replace or omit estimates; they do not add participants.

## Interpretation and stopping scope

No synthesis meets the project preference of five comparable independent studies. This is acknowledged, not repaired by adding recognition outcomes, hearing simulations, qualitative reports or unknown group sizes. The three-cohort association synthesis is a conditional, exploratory analysis, not causal evidence of reading instruction effectiveness. The empirical title and scope therefore emphasize classroom-related comprehension and an evidence review, rather than asserting an exhaustive systematic review or causal effects of a universal teaching format.

The missing five first-batch originals are P14, P18, P19, P23 and P24. The user supplied all purchasable originals. This delivered analysis uses the available corpus; no further purchase is required for its stated scope. A future update with those sources could change the selection, estimate and title.
